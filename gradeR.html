Art & Design

Kids will explore various ways to be artistic and express themselves. Engaging in enjoyable activities like drawing and crafting, they'll enhance their imagination and fine motor skills. Additionally, they'll discover different art forms and learn basic concepts of color, shape, and composition.

Meditation

Kids will learn easy ways to be calm and focused. They'll practice simple breathing and relaxation exercises to help them feel peaceful and control their feelings. With help, they'll understand how paying attention to the present moment can make them feel better and more relaxed.

Games

Kids will have fun while learning through play with different activities. They'll play games and solve puzzles to get better at thinking and understanding space. Also, they'll learn to work with others, talk nicely, and be good sports, making sure everyone feels welcome and happy to learn together.

Development

Kids will do things that help them grow in many ways. They'll play games to get better at moving their bodies and working together. They'll also learn how to realize and share feelings with others, and they'll solve puzzles and listen to stories to get better at thinking and figuring things out.

Audio & Music

Children will start an exciting journey of exploring sounds and rhythms. They'll listen to different sounds and learn about music in fun ways. They'll also learn about things like rhythm, melody, and how music works, which will make them want to be creative and express themselves through music.

Cleanliness

In our Grade R Cleanliness program, children will learn important habits like washing hands and keeping shared spaces clean through fun activities. By instilling these habits early, we aim to promote their health and responsibility for themselves and their environment.
