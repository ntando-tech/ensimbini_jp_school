Frequently Asked Question

You May Ask

Does the school charge any admission fees?
No, Ensimbini Primary School is a public school and does not charge any admission fees.
What are the admission requirements for new students?
New students who have not previously failed at another school are eligible for admission. Contact the school directly for more information and specific requirements.
What is the policy on transfer students?
The school can consider transfer students. However, acceptance depends on available space and whether the student meets the admission criteria, including not having previously failed at another school.
What was the school's most recent grade 7 pass rate?
The school's most recent pass rate was 92%.
What subjects are offered at the school?
Grade 4 - 7 : IsiZulu,English,Maths,Life Orientation,Natural Science,Technology,Social Science,E.M.S
What support is available for students who need extra help?
The school offers various support programs, including after-school tutoring and academic interventions.
Where can I buy the school uniform?
The school uniform is sold at Holiday Makers store.
What is the school dress code?
Boys: black school Tie, light-blue shirt, black trousers, gray socks, black shoes.
Girls: light-blue shirt, black skirt or blue & white tunic, blue socks, tie, black shoes.
Hairstyles: Boys: brush cut or chiskop (girls can also have these styles). Girls: various styles allowed.
Does the school offer any extracurricular activities or clubs?
Yes, the school offers a variety of extracurricular activities and clubs. Soccer,Netball,Olympic
How can parents get involved in the school community?
The school encourages parental involvement through various means, including parent-teacher associations (PTAs) and school governing bodies (SGBs). Contact the school for more information on how to get involved.
